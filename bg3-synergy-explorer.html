BG3 Synergy Explorer

Find the gear that works together.

Description

A web app that helps Baldur's Gate 3 players choose gear that works together, by organizing items around the mechanics they apply and pay off from.

  • The problem

    Choosing gear in Baldur's Gate 3 is overwhelming because items rarely matter on their own. They matter because they feed shared mechanics, conditions like Radiating Orb or Lightning Charges. One item applies the condition and another gets stronger from it, but the game never shows you which pieces belong together.

  • The approach

    Instead of starting with raw stats, every item in the data model records which mechanics it touches and how: it applies them, benefits from them, or both. Pick a mechanic to see every source and payoff, grouped by slot. Equip items into a loadout that follows the game's slot rules, and a synergy check shows which mechanics are fully hooked up, which are applied with nothing cashing in, and which payoffs have nothing triggering them, with suggestions to fill the gaps. The starter set covers 28 items across four mechanics. Each item was checked against its wiki page, and items where sources disagree are flagged instead of quietly kept or dropped.
